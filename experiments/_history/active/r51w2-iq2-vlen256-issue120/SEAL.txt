--- ROOT CAUSE ---
emitIQ2XSSuperBlockGridBody / emitIQ2SSuperBlockGridBody (lib/Conversion/RVV/RVVToEmitCGridCodebook.cpp):
the pair-batched grid decode does ONE wide i64m4 gather + i8m4 vmul sign-fold (4 halves of 16
lanes each land at element offsets 0/16/32/48 of the i8m4 group at ANY VLEN), then recovered
each half with __riscv_vget_v_i8m4_i8m1(v, ph). vget(.,ph) reads register-subgroup ph =
element window [ph*VLEN/8, ...) = [16*ph,..) at VLEN128 (correct) but [32*ph,..) at VLEN256
(WRONG lanes). => VLEN256 byte-broken (mism=512), VLEN128 fine.

--- FIX (owned C-intrinsic, no inline-asm; VLEN-universal single body) ---
Recover half ph VLEN-AGNOSTICALLY: vslidedown the wide i8m4 by the LITERAL element offset
16*ph (ph=1..3; ph=0 no slide) so the half lands at lane 0, then vget(.,0) extracts the low
i8m1 register (lanes [0,16) = the half at every VLEN). Per-half vwmul_i16m2 + vwredsum
UNCHANGED (AVL=16). Same value at VLEN128 (slide-then-get0 == get(.,ph)); correct at VLEN256+.
Same pattern already used for VLEN256 correctness in RVVToEmitCKQuant.cpp (aux32 fold-back)
and RVVToEmitCForwardElementwise.cpp (bsums chunking). Applied to BOTH iq2_xs and iq2_s.

--- GATE (byte-exact ZERO-MODEL, correctness only, no perf) ---
driver = iq2_vecdot_driver_ext.c (extends block2-p2 driver with iq2_s: oracle_iq2_s +
iq2s_grid[1024] in iq2_oracle_tables_ext.h). 3-way: ours vs int-oracle (ZERO-MODEL primary)
vs ggml-deployed. INT-mode bounded fill => order-independent => byte-exact certificate.
